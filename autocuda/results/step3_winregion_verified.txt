MECHANISM (proposal's thesis, confirmed): powerlaw = multi-hot warm set that overflows the cache;
the residual spill is frequent AND contended across blocks/channels on the shared output. Multi-channel
amplifies (3 channels race the same output bins). Contention-free private spill wins there. But the
cache already absorbs the hottest bins, so on concentrated (1 hot bin, fully cached) and hash_synonym
(collisions handled) priv's gather tax is pure loss.

UNEXPLOITABLE by the selector: select_algorithm cannot observe input shape, so routing
(multi_even, 65536-262144) to priv would also capture concentrated/hash_synonym inputs and regress them
15-57%. Net over the shape mix is negative. => keep unselected.
